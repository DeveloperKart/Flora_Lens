About FloraLens

AI meets the world of plants.

FloraLens is an educational AI project created using Google Teachable Machine. The model has been trained to recognize three medicinal plants and one weed.

The system demonstrates how machine learning can be trained using images and then used in a real-world camera application.

🧠

Machine Learning

The model learns visual patterns from example images and uses them to classify new images.

📷

Live Camera

The trained model receives frames from your camera and continuously analyzes them.

🌱

Plant Awareness

The project demonstrates how AI can help people explore and learn about plants.

⚠️ Important: FloraLens is an educational AI demonstration. Predictions may be incorrect, especially with poor lighting, background clutter, unusual camera angles, motion or noisy visual environments. Do not consume or use a plant based solely on an AI prediction. Always verify plant identity with a reliable source or qualified expert.
